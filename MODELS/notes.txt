MATH NEEDED:

**Core Probability and Statistics**

* Probability rules, conditional probability, independence
* Distributions: Normal, Poisson, Binomial, Exponential
* Expected value, variance and correlation
* Law of large numbers, central limit theorem, Bayes Theorem
* Hypothesis testing and confidence intervals
* Regression basics (linear, logistic)
* Bayesian Thinking
  - Prior, liklihood, posterior
  - Bayesian updating
  - Monte Carlo sampling for posterior simulation
* Decision and Risk Theory
  - Expected Utility and risk aversion
  - Kelly Criterion
  - Value at Risk(VaR) and expected shortfall
  - Correlated Risk and portfolio variance
* Stochastic Processes
  - Markov Chain
  - Martingales
  - Random Walks and Brownian motion

**Applied Statistics for Sports**

* Time series analysis (ARIMA, moving averages, stationarity)
* Correlation and autocorrelation
* Survival analysis (player time on court, injury risk modeling)
* Bayesian inference (updating beliefs as new games are played)

**Machine Learning Foundations**

* Linear regression, logistic regression
* Decision trees, random forests, gradient boosting (XGBoost, CatBoost, LightGBM)
* Regularization (L1, L2)
* Feature engineering for temporal and categorical data

**Optimization and Decision Making**

* Monte Carlo simulation for scenario testing
* Expected value (EV) and Kelly Criterion for bankroll sizing
* Linear programming and constrained optimization (useful in DFS lineups)

**Mathematics of the Game**

* Combinatorics (lineup combinations, possession outcomes)
* Markov chains (possession-based modeling, win probability)
* Elo/Glicko rating systems (team and player strength)

**Advanced/Optional**

* Calculus (to understand optimization and ML gradients)
* Linear algebra (to understand matrix operations in ML models)
* Probability density estimation (kernel density, bootstrapping)
