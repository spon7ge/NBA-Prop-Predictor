NBA Prop Predictor

Last updated:

This dashboard analyzes NBA player props from PrizePicks and Underdog using a probabilistic model, highlighting players with the highest probability of either hitting or missing the prop line.

Note: Don't focus too much on the exact projected numbers. NBA games have inherent uncertainty, making it difficult to predict precisely how a player will perform on any given night.

Currently Only Offering Point Props

It uses past performance data to predict a range of possible outcomes for each player. It estimates how often a player will go over or under the prop line. It then compares those probabilities with the odds you get from the sportsbook.

📈 Other Props

Select "Over Hit Rates" to view historical statistics for assists, rebounds, blocks, steals, and combo props.Would recommend using this tool in conjunction with other sources to make your final decision.

💡 Tip: Best used 15 minutes before tipoff after lineups are released.
