FAQ

Frequently asked questions about the platform

What is HoopVista?

HoopVista is a research tool for NBA DFS player props. It surfaces model projections, expected value, hit-rate context, and ranked pair ideas so you can evaluate props with more than a gut feel.

What sports do you cover?

We focus on NBA basketball—player props such as points, rebounds, assists, and related markets for the current slate.

What sportsbooks do you support?

Top Pairs and exports are set up for PrizePicks, Underdog, DraftKings Pick 6, and Betr. Use the Book menu on Top Pairs to switch slates.
